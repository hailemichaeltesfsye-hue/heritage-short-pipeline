# Ethical AI Framework & Governance: The Heritage Short

## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose
"AI-Content Generation: The Heritage Short" is a narrative film celebrating Ethiopian heritage, personal resilience, and cultural legacy through an autobiographical account titled *"Hulum Neger Lebego New (Everything for Good)"*. This document codifies ethical AI principles, biometric representation permissions, data privacy protections, and safety controls guiding every stage of production.

---

## 2. Explicit Self-Consent Record: Hailemichael Tesfaye
- **Individual**: Hailemichael Tesfaye
- **Role**: Subject, Author, and Protagonist of the Short Film
- **Consent Type**: Explicit, informed, written self-consent for biographical portrayal
- **Scope of Representation**:
  - Depiction of personal life milestones: childhood in Delanta Wereda (Wegeltena), schooling at Mesno Amba Primary, relocation to Kombolcha, surviving childhood appendicitis surgery, traditional Abinet theological training and deacon ordination, academic transition to Wollo University, and current status as a 3rd-year Software Engineering student.
  - Generation of AI stylized visual likenesses representing Hailemichael across childhood, adolescence, and early adulthood.
  - Use of Amharic narration reflecting genuine lived experiences and personal philosophy.
- **Revocation Protocol**: The subject retains unconditioned authority to request alteration, re-generation, or removal of any depiction at any milestone in production.

---

## 3. Privacy Protection Policy for Family Members & Non-Depicted Individuals
- **Non-Depiction of Private Persons**: Family members (including the subject's mother, relatives, teachers, and peers) are represented strictly through respectful, anonymous contextual narrative framing.
- **Biometric Protection**: No real photographic facial reference, biometric scan, or identifiable voice clone of any living third party is trained upon, uploaded to external APIs, or synthesized.
- **Symbolic & Archetypal Portrayal**: Secondary figures appear solely as generalized cinematic silhouettes or documentary archetypes (e.g., "a supportive mother's hand", "courtyard classmates in school uniforms") ensuring complete privacy and zero individual identification.

---

## 4. AI Disclosure & Provenance Transparency
- **C2PA & Synthetic Media Transparency**: All output audio tracks and video renders incorporate synthetic media metadata disclosures.
- **Labeling Notice**: End credits and container metadata explicitly state:
  > *"This film is an autobiographical artistic work created with human-directed AI pipeline technology. Narration, character progression visuals, and score were synthesized under direct human supervision."*

---

## 5. Cultural Integrity & Respect for Heritage
- **Language Fidelity**: Amharic narration (`narration_am`) and Ge'ez theological contexts (Abinet schooling, liturgical Deaconhood) are authored with linguistic dignity and cultural authenticity.
- **Stereotype Avoidance**: Depictions avoid poverty pornography, western-savior tropes, or caricature, highlighting the dignity, scenic beauty, and intellectual dynamism of the Ethiopian highlands.

---

## 6. Secret Leaks & API Security
- Zero credentials, tokens, or private endpoints are permitted in version control.
- Automated static checks enforce secret elimination prior to commit and deployment.
